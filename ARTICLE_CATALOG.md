# Article Catalog — hollowpipa.besi.co.id

Scope: 96 non-geographic article briefs for an Indonesian hollow-section and steel-pipe knowledge system. The catalog supports, but does not replace, the existing commercial product routes.

Domain boundaries: articles explain terminology, decisions, evidence, workflow, failure prevention, and stop conditions. They do not certify products, approve structural/pressure designs, prescribe project-specific welding or testing parameters, or make undated stock/price claims. City-swapped briefs are prohibited. Similar articles on other owned domains are not cannibalization; only overlap within `hollowpipa.besi.co.id` is controlled here.

Catalog statistics: 16 parent topics; 96 briefs; six briefs per topic; 96 unique planned slugs; 12 Wave 1 assets.

Priority legend: `P0` establishes the minimum safe knowledge spine; `P1` completes common selection and execution journeys; `P2` deepens specialist and diagnostic coverage; `P3` is useful long-tail or advanced support. Waves are bounded publication groups, not a command to publish everything at once.

## First-wave publication standard

Wave 1 is the 12-row P0 set. Publish it as one connected cluster: the orientation hub; terminology; manufacturing route; dimension and mass tools; certificate literacy; two major product comparisons; corrosion fundamentals; structural and piping input checklists; and connection selection.

Before release, every asset needs a verified source pack, named technical reviewer where required, custom diagram/tool/checklist, explicit last-reviewed date for volatile standards, upward hub link, contextual lateral links, and a correct next step. Monitor indexation, impressions by distinct intent, task/tool use, specification quality in enquiries, qualified leads, and URL/query oscillation. Do not begin Wave 2 merely because all Wave 1 drafts exist; require evidence that the cluster is technically reviewed, internally linked, indexed as intended, and not cannibalizing the eight product routes.
