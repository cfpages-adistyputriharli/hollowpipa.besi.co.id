# Topical Authority — hollowpipa.besi.co.id

## Role and boundary

`hollowpipa.besi.co.id` should become an Indonesian reference for selecting, specifying, procuring, fabricating, installing, inspecting, and maintaining steel hollow sections and pipe. The site may support qualified commercial enquiries, but technical education must remain separate from sales claims and must not imply that one product is safe for every structural, pressure, sanitary, chemical, or high-temperature duty.

This plan covers non-geographic knowledge. City-, regency-, and province-swapped sales pages are existing inventory to audit, not a model for new articles. Structural capacity, pressure rating, welding procedure, fluid compatibility, and code compliance require project-specific engineering or qualified review; articles may explain the decision process but may not approve a design.

## Internal-link architecture

- `HP-01-01` is the neutral orientation hub and links to all 16 topic hubs; the home page may link to it as “Panduan Hollow & Pipa”.
- Each topic hub links to its six children and every child links back to its topic hub.
- Selection pages in HP-05 link backward to vocabulary, manufacturing, dimensions, grade evidence, and corrosion; they link forward to structural or piping engineering gates.
- Structural HP-07 and piping HP-08 pages link to joining HP-09, routing HP-11, installation HP-12, QA HP-13, and safety HP-16 only where the relationship is real.
- Diagnostic HP-14 pages link to prevention in HP-06/HP-10/HP-12, evidence in HP-13, and repair/replace stop conditions.
- Procurement HP-15 pages link to HP-03 for quantity, HP-04 for traceability, HP-13 for receiving evidence, and only then to the relevant commercial product route.
- Commercial hubs may receive contextual links from a selection or procurement page after the reader has identified the required family; informational pages must not be rewritten as disguised sales pages.
- No article is published until its upward hub link, two or more useful lateral links, and next-step link are defined.

## Evidence and editorial standards

- Verify current SNI, ISO, ASTM, ASME/API, AWS, manufacturer, and Indonesian K3/legal requirements from primary sources before naming a designation or clause. Record edition and access date.
- Never infer grade equivalence from similar names. State the required chemical, mechanical, dimensional, testing, and certification evidence.
- Do not calculate or imply allowable structural load, pressure, temperature, support spacing, corrosion allowance, weld acceptance, or safe continued service without qualified project-specific review.
- Separate nominal dimensions from measured dimensions and theoretical mass from certified or weighed mass. Show assumptions and units.
- Prices, stock, lead time, factory-origin, warranty, and delivery claims require dated commercial evidence; do not hard-code unsourced prices into evergreen education.
- Health/safety pages require competent K3 review, SDS/primary guidance, stop conditions, and clear responsibility boundaries.
- Use original or licensed diagrams/photos. Defect images need known context; do not diagnose safety from a generic stock photo.
- Case studies require real project records and permission. Label illustrative scenarios as scenarios, not field experience.

Success signals:

- all 12 URLs are indexable, included in the intended sitemap, and internally reachable within three clicks;

- readers use diagrams, calculators/checklists, or move to the correct product/engineering next step;

- no planned page displaces the correct commercial hub for transactional queries;
- quarterly review finds no title/slug duplication, shared-outline drift, unsupported claims, or orphan pages.
